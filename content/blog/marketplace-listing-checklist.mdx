A useful marketplace listing helps a buyer understand exactly what is offered, compare it with alternatives and assess delivery or returns. Use accurate titles, product details and images, then answer practical questions about variations and fulfilment. Keep the information consistent with your actual stock and policies, and review marketplace requirements before publication.

## What information does a buyer need?

A buyer needs to know what the item is, whether it fits their requirements and what will happen after ordering. Depending on the product, that may include dimensions, materials, compatibility, condition, variations and what is included in the package.

A **product attribute** is a specific characteristic of an item, such as its size or material. Use the marketplace fields intended for attributes rather than leaving essential information only in a long description.

## How should you review a listing?

Read the listing as someone unfamiliar with the product. Check whether its title, images, variations and description agree. Remove unsupported claims and clarify information that could mean different things to different buyers.

| Listing element | Practical check                                           |
| --------------- | --------------------------------------------------------- |
| Title           | Does it accurately identify the product?                  |
| Images          | Do they show the actual item and relevant details?        |
| Variations      | Can the buyer distinguish the options?                    |
| Delivery        | Are the stated arrangements accurate?                     |
| Returns         | Is the information consistent with the applicable policy? |

### What makes images useful?

Show details a buyer needs to evaluate, with photographs you have permission to use. Avoid suggesting that accessories are included when they are only part of the scene. Plan image accessibility and text alternatives where the platform allows them.

## How does shop setup differ from listing optimisation?

Shop setup organises the overall presence, including categories and customer-facing information. Listing optimisation improves the information attached to an individual product. A well-organised shop does not compensate for inaccurate product details, and a strong listing still needs reliable fulfilment.

Make a checklist for each product type so updates are repeatable. Keep a record of where the authoritative specifications, stock and policies live.

## How should you choose the next improvement?

Start with issues that make the product difficult to understand or buy. Correct inaccurate details and unclear variations before experimenting with wording. Review changes alongside the available marketplace data without assuming that one change caused every sales movement.
